# PROPERTYXAI CEO Workspace

## Purpose
A single executive operating view for the PROPERTYXAI marketplace, AI intelligence products, trust operations, supply growth, and enterprise delivery.

## CEO weekly operating rhythm
- Monday: supply, demand, conversion, revenue, and market coverage.
- Tuesday: product quality, search relevance, AI evaluation, and customer feedback.
- Wednesday: trust queue, fraud patterns, verification SLA, and compliance exceptions.
- Thursday: partnerships, agent/developer pipeline, and data licensing.
- Friday: engineering delivery, reliability, security, cash/runway, and next-week priorities.

## Dashboard modules
1. Company pulse: active users, active verified listings, qualified inquiries, revenue, conversion, availability.
2. Marketplace liquidity: listings by country/city/category, freshness, time-to-first-inquiry, zero-result searches.
3. Trust command: pending verification, reported listings, duplicate/anomaly queue, appeals, resolution SLA.
4. AI quality: task volume, success, latency, cost, user ratings, human override, confidence calibration, model drift.
5. Growth: acquisition channel, CAC, activation, retention, agent/developer pipeline, referral contribution.
6. Enterprise: organizations, seats, plan mix, usage, API consumption, renewals, support SLA.
7. Engineering: deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate, incident count, P95 latency, error budget.
8. Finance: MRR/ARR, gross margin, infrastructure and model spend, collections, runway.

## CEO decision rules
- Do not expand into a country until data rights, local legal review, verification operations, and support coverage are in place.
- Do not label an estimate as a valuation unless methodology, coverage, calibration, and uncertainty are visible.
- Do not grant enterprise access without tenant isolation, least-privilege roles, auditability, and tested offboarding.
- Do not reward listing volume without freshness and verification quality.
- Review AI cost per successful task, not only total token spend.
